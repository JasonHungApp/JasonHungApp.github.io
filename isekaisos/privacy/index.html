最後更新：2026-09-19｜開發者：YU PEI LIN / Jason Hung 工作室

我們收集什麼

遊戲沒有帳號系統，也沒有自己的伺服器。開發者不會收集、儲存或取得你的個人資料。

  • 遊戲進度：牌組、解鎖內容、統計與設定只存在你裝置的本機檔案裡，不會上傳。解除安裝 App 即一併刪除。

廣告

App 使用 Google AdMob 顯示廣告（例如觀看廣告換取鑰匙）。AdMob 可能會收集並使用下列資料，用於投放廣告、衡量成效與防止濫用：

  • 裝置識別碼（例如廣告 ID）
  • IP 位址與大致地理位置
  • 裝置與 App 的使用資訊、廣告互動紀錄

這些資料由 Google 依其隱私權政策處理，開發者無法取得。詳見 Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式中的資料。

你的選擇

  • 在歐洲經濟區、英國與瑞士，首次開啟時會出現同意表單，由你決定是否同意廣告使用裝置識別碼。事後可在遊戲「設定」面板按「廣告隱私權選項」重新調整。
  • 你也可以在裝置系統設定中限制廣告追蹤或重設廣告 ID（iOS：設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 追蹤；Android：設定 → Google → 廣告）。

兒童

本遊戲並非以 13 歲以下兒童為對象。我們不會刻意收集兒童的個人資料。

網頁版

網頁試玩版不含廣告，進度存於瀏覽器本機儲存空間，不會傳送到任何伺服器。

政策變更

政策若有更動，會更新本頁並修改上方日期。

Isekai SOS: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-19 | Developer: YU PEI LIN / Jason Hung Studio

What we collect

The game has no accounts and no server of its own. The developer does not collect, store, or receive your personal data.

  • Game progress: your deck, unlocks, stats and settings are stored only in a local file on your device and are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to show ads (for example, watching an ad to earn keys). AdMob may collect and use the following to serve ads, measure performance and prevent abuse:

  • Device identifiers (such as the advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • App and device usage information, and ad interactions

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy, and the developer cannot access it. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choices

  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, a consent form appears on first launch so you can decide whether ads may use device identifiers. You can change this later via "Ad Privacy Options" in the game's Settings panel.
  • You can also limit ad tracking or reset your advertising ID in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

Children

This game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Web version

The browser demo contains no ads. Progress is kept in your browser's local storage and is not sent to any server.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the date above will be updated.
