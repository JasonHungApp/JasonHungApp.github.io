Isekai SOS

Roguelike deck-builder · iOS / Android / Web

A fast, minimalist roguelike deck-builder. Prepare at the camp by opening chests and choosing 1 of 3 cards, then face the boss who is draining the kingdom's power. Only three numbers to manage: Health, Armor and Attack.

WaterPurr

Water reminder · Health & Fitness · iOS / Android

Set your daily number of cups. Every cup you drink lowers the water and reveals a cute picture hidden underneath. Keep your streak to collect cards and unlock seasonal effects. Supports home screen widgets and multiple languages.
