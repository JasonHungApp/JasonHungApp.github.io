WaterPurr Privacy Policy

Developer: YU PEI LIN / Jason Hung Studio

Welcome to "WaterPurr"! We highly value your privacy, which is why this app is specially designed to protect the security of your data. This Privacy Policy explains in detail how we handle your personal information.

1. Data Collection and Use

"WaterPurr" does not collect or store users' personal data on backend servers. All data (e.g., daily water intake records, reminder settings) is stored solely on your device and remains fully under your control.

2. Data Security

Since we do not transmit data to the cloud or other servers, your personal information stays on your device, reducing the risk of leaks. We recommend keeping your device secure to prevent data loss or unauthorized access.

3. Permission Usage

This app may request certain device permissions (e.g., notification permissions) to provide the best user experience. These permissions are used only for the app's core functions and not for any other purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

"WaterPurr" does not use any third-party services to collect or process your personal data.

5. Changes to the Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as needed. You will be notified of updates through the app, so please review the latest version of the policy.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us anytime: Email: apprdteam@gmail.com
